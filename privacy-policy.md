# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Star Battle app for Apple devices, such as iPhone, iPad and Mac, and any related services operated by Andrei Ardelean (collectively, the "Application"). Andrei Ardelean is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

**In short**

The Application has no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. The Service Provider runs no server for it, and never sees, collects or sells your progress, your settings or any other personal information, apart from what you choose to send, such as a support message, or to share as a beta tester. Your progress stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.

**What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?**

The Application does not collect, log, or send any personal information to the Service Provider. Registration is not required.

The puzzles you have started, solved or skipped, your marks on the board, your stats and streak, and your settings are stored on your device, so the Application can bring them back the next time you play. The Application does not connect to any server run by the Service Provider; it only uses Apple's services, such as the App Store and iCloud, described below.

**iCloud**

If iCloud is turned on for Star Battle (the "Sync with iCloud" switch in Settings, on by default), your progress (the state of each puzzle you have played, such as its stars and marks, and whether it is solved or skipped) is stored in your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, and your settings are stored using iCloud's key-value storage, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This data belongs to your iCloud account; the Service Provider has no access to it. [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) covers how Apple stores it. You can turn off iCloud for Star Battle at any time with the switch in Settings or in your device's settings (the Settings app on iPhone and iPad, or System Settings on Mac).

**Purchases**

The optional Star Battle Unlimited purchase is processed by Apple. The Service Provider never receives your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. Whether you own Unlimited is checked on your device through Apple's StoreKit. Apple provides the Service Provider with sales reports that do not identify you.

**Notifications**

The Application does not show notifications and does not ask for permission to send them. It receives silent signals from Apple's services only so that iCloud can tell your devices when your progress has changed.

**Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?**

This Application does not collect any information about the location of your device. It does not use the camera, your photos, your contacts or any other personal data on your device.

**Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?**

The Application contains no third-party code for analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and no data is sold. The only parties other than you and the Service Provider that may handle your data are Apple, which provides the services the Application relies on (the App Store, in-app purchases and iCloud) under its own privacy policy, and Apple iCloud Mail, which receives your messages as described under Support messages.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with the Service Provider, in a form that does not identify you. You can turn this off at any time in your device's settings.

**How do I delete my data?**

"Reset Progress" in Settings sets every solved, started and skipped puzzle back to new, on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your iCloud account and on your other devices. "Reset Settings" returns your settings to their defaults.

Uninstalling the Application removes the data stored on that device, but not the copy in your iCloud account, which returns if you install the Application again. To remove that copy, use "Reset Progress" first, or delete Star Battle's data from your iCloud storage, which you can manage from your Apple Account in your device's settings. Copies may also remain in backups of your device, such as iCloud Backup or a backup on a computer, until those backups are deleted or replaced.

If you contact the Service Provider directly, you may request deletion of that correspondence at any time by writing via [nobler-mentees9j@icloud.com](mailto:nobler-mentees9j@icloud.com).

**Support messages**

Writing to the Service Provider is optional and not needed to use the Application. If you write, your message, the name or address you write from, and anything you choose to include are used only to answer you and to fix the problems you report. The legal basis is the Service Provider's legitimate interest in responding to you, supporting the Application and protecting its legal rights (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), or, for questions about a purchase, providing the service you bought (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Messages are received and stored by Apple iCloud Mail, which handles them under Apple's own privacy policy. That service may store and process them outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards it provides for international transfers, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. The Service Provider does not share your messages with anyone else. They are kept as long as needed to answer you and to follow up on what you reported, and after that for as long as they may be needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, such as a dispute about a purchase. This may be 12 months or more after the conversation ends, but generally no longer than the limitation period that applies to such claims. You can ask for them to be deleted at any time; the Service Provider will delete them unless they are still needed for a legal claim.

**Beta testing**

If you test pre-release versions of the Application through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares some information about you with the Service Provider: depending on how you were invited, your name and email address, as well as your device model, operating system version, crash reports, and any feedback and screenshots you choose to send. This information is used only to test and improve the Application, on the basis of the Service Provider's legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). It is kept while you remain a tester, and feedback for as long as it is needed to fix what it reports. You can stop testing at any time in the TestFlight app.

**Your rights**

Because the Service Provider holds no personal data from the Application, there is nothing to access, correct or export beyond what you see in the Application itself. For anything you have sent the Service Provider directly, or that Apple has shared about you as a beta tester, you have the right to access it, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, and receive a copy of it, by writing via [nobler-mentees9j@icloud.com](mailto:nobler-mentees9j@icloud.com). If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

The Application makes no automated decisions about you and does no profiling.

**Children**

The Application does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

**Security**

Your progress is protected by your device's own security, such as its passcode or password and data protection, and, when stored in iCloud, by Apple's iCloud security. Keep your device locked with a passcode or password to keep your data private.

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, by posting the updated Privacy Policy on this page with a new effective date. If the Application ever starts collecting any data, this policy will say exactly what is collected and why before that change is released.

Previous versions of this Privacy Policy are available on request via [nobler-mentees9j@icloud.com](mailto:nobler-mentees9j@icloud.com).
